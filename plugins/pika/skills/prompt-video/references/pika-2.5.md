# Pika 2.5 — Prompt Guide

Pika 2.5 turns photos into dynamic, high-fidelity videos, and also generates video from a text prompt alone. It works in two ways: animate an existing image, or describe a scene from scratch. When you animate an image, the source image carries the subject — the picture is what appears on screen — and the text prompt guides the motion. When you generate from text alone, the prompt describes the entire video.

## Prompt structure
- **When animating an image** — the image already establishes the subject, wardrobe, and setting, so write the prompt as a description of the *motion*: what moves, how, and how the camera behaves. Keep the described action consistent with what is already in the frame.
- **When generating from text** — describe the full scene as confident, painterly prose: the subject, wardrobe and surroundings, the mood, the color grade, and the framing. Declare the composition in words.

## Style & craft
Pika 2.5's own house style favors richly specified, editorial prose. Name the subject and wardrobe in precise detail, describe the surrounding environment, then set the overall visual treatment — silhouette clarity, painterly versus photographic feel, saturation, and film grade. Declare the composition in words (for example, "Square 1:1 composition") rather than assuming it. Close with clean-plate exclusions to keep the frame free of unwanted marks.

## Example prompt (text-to-video, house style)
> "A surreal high-fashion full-figure portrait of a striking model with short cropped spiky black hair, seated on the mossy overgrown limbs of a twisting sculptural branch in a mountain meadow under a vivid blue sky with puffy white clouds. She wears a chartreuse-green knit mini turtleneck dress with matching long gloves, layered pearl necklaces, tall white knee socks and silver heeled shoes. Around her float oversized felted-wool flowers in coral, salmon and dusty pink on thorned vines. Clear silhouette, editorial and painterly, saturated and dreamlike. Warm analog film grade, lifted milky blacks, pigmented (not neon) midtones, fine 35mm grain and halation, premium retro-futurist creative-studio look. Square 1:1 composition. No text, no logos, no wordmarks, no watermarks."

## Notes
Pika 2.5's published guidance is intentionally light on rules. The core craft is: let the image carry the subject and use the prompt for motion when animating a still; write full, editorial, materially specific prose when generating from text; declare composition in words; and close with clean-plate exclusions ("no text, no logos, no wordmarks, no watermarks").
